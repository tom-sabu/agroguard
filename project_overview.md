# AgriLocal: Hyper-Local Marketplace for Kottayam

AgriLocal is a specialized marketplace designed for home farmers in Kottayam. It aims to bridge the gap between local producers and buyers by providing a simple, map-based interface to discover and sell fresh produce.

## Problem
Home farmers often have surplus produce (rubber, coconuts, pepper, fruits) but lack a dedicated platform to reach nearby buyers without intermediaries.

## Solution
A lightweight, mobile-friendly web app where:
1. Sellers can quickly post listings with photos and location.
2. Buyers can see what's available nearby on an interactive map.
3. Users can check current market trends for major crops in the region.

## Target Audience
- Home farmers and backyard gardeners in Kottayam.
- Local residents looking for fresh, farm-direct produce.
